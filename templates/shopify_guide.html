Shopify 5-Step Visual Setup Guide

Follow these 5 easy steps with screenshots to paste your RankSleep SEO code into your Shopify store. Once saved, our autonomous agent runs 24/7 on daily autopilot.

1

Step 1: Open "Online Store" in Shopify Admin

In your Shopify Admin dashboard, navigate to the left sidebar menu and click on Online Store (under the Sales channels section).

2

Step 2: Click the Three Dots (...) & Select "Edit code"

Next to your active live theme, click on the three dots (...) button to open the menu, then select Edit code from the dropdown options.

3

Step 3: Search for "theme.liquid"

In the top search bar inside the code editor, type: theme.liquid

4

Step 4: Select "theme.liquid" from Search Results

Click on the theme.liquid (layout) file from the search dropdown results to open the file in the editor window.

5

Step 5: Paste the Code & Click "Save" — That's All!

Final Step

Paste your copied RankSleep SEO snippet right under the <head> tag (or right above </head>), then click the blue Save button in the top right.

🎉 Setup Complete! You are done! RankSleep SEO is now active and will continuously optimize your store 24/7 on daily autopilot.
