Official Shopify Partner Integration RankSleep connects directly via modern Shopify Admin APIs (REST & GraphQL) to read product catalogs, write high-converting Gemini descriptions, and inject Schema.org JSON-LD structured data.
1
Automatic 1-Click OAuth Sync (Recommended) Type your store address (e.g. outfitoss.myshopify.com) into the scan bar above and click Connect Store. You will be taken to Shopify's secure app authorization page.
2
Custom App Admin API Configuration In your Shopify Admin → Settings → Apps and sales channels → Develop apps. Ensure write_products and read_products scopes are granted.
3
Autonomous Overnight Ranking Crawl Toggle the Autopilot Live switch. RankSleep continuously optimizes new SKUs, fixes title truncations, and pings search indexers automatically.
